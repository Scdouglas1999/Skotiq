## 4. User-facing copy audit

Grepped every `Skotiq`/`skotiq` string literal in `packages/*/lib`,
`apps/*/lib`, `lib` (~440 prose hits). Findings:

- Product name is `Skotiq` (capital S) in sentences everywhere; lowercase
  `skotiq` appears only in identifiers, paths, hostnames and URLs
  (`skotiq.local`, `skotiq.db`, `skotiq/...` channels, `com.skotiq.*`).
- `'SKOTIQ'` in `title_bar.dart:142` and `_mobile_settings_bar.dart:45` is the
  all-caps wordmark with `letterSpacing` styling — intentional treatment, kept.
- No remnants of the old name's wordplay: searched `deadly`, `<old>`,
  `belladonna`, `botanical`, `plant`, `garden`, `shade` —
  only innocuous hits (`poison the fit`, `a shade darker`, `two shades of one
  hue`).
- About page (`about_settings.dart`), onboarding (`welcome_step.dart`),
  license card, Spanish translations (`translations.dart`, `es` locale) all
  read correctly with the new name.
- Nothing user-facing explains the name; no copy needed rewriting for sense.
