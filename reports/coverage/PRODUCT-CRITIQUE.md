# Skotiq sweep — product critique

Not defects. These are places the app works as built and the design is wrong: a default everyone must change, a flow that costs more clicks than it should, something a user reaches for and does not find. Refuted findings are excluded.

Generated by `tools/production/findings_report.dart` from `reports/coverage/swept`.

- **431** distinct findings across **17** areas (431 raw, 0 duplicates merged)
- **2** independently reproduced by an adversarial verifier; **26** were refuted and are excluded
- **70** units recorded as unreached, with reasons

### Fail-closed is the shipped default and the Weather Safety page never tells a sensorless rig what that means

`default` · settings (sequencer, science, phd2-guiding, plate-solving, weather-safety, notifications + all transports, integrations/plugins, annotations, observation log, auto-save, cloud-sync) · **CONFIRMED** _(reported P1, corrected by verifier)_

**What happens:** safety_fail_mode defaults to failClosed (verified in the fresh DB and at app_settings.dart:82). 'Enable weather safety' correctly defaults OFF -- but the moment a user turns it on, which reads like the responsible thing to do, a rig with no weather device and no safety monitor is treated as unsafe: the app raises a CRITICAL 'Safe the rig', and per the codebase's own WeatherSafetyNoSourceRule 'the run will park and abort shortly after it starts'. The Weather Safety page itself contains NO fail-mode control, NO row showing which weather sources are connected or what they currently read, and NO hint that zero sources are attached. The fail-mode dropdown lives on a different page (Sequencer). The rule's resolution hint literally says 'switch the safety fail mode to fail open in Settings > Automation & Safety' -- a string the user only meets at run-start, not on the page where they made the choice.

**Expected:** The page that owns the master switch should show live source state and the fail-mode consequence. Concretely: a status row at the top ('Weather sources: none connected -- with Fail Closed, runs will park and abort'), the fail-mode selector moved next to the master switch, and turning the master switch on with zero sources should either default that rig to Warn Only or ask.

**Repro:** Fresh profile, nothing connected. Settings > AUTOMATION & SAFETY > Weather Safety. Read the whole page (3 toggles, 3 thresholds, 2 alert-timing numbers) -- nothing mentions sources or fail mode. Toggle 'Enable weather safety' on. Then go to Sequencer to find 'Safety fail mode: Fail Closed (Park)'.

**Where:** `packages/skotiq_app/lib/screens/settings/widgets/weather_safety_settings.dart:120-175; default at packages/skotiq_core/lib/src/models/settings/app_settings.dart:82; consequence documented at packages/skotiq_core/lib/src/providers/sequence/rules/weather_safety_rules.dart:9-70`

**Proposed fix:** Add a source-status SettingRow driven by weatherStateProvider/safetyMonitorStateProvider at the top of WeatherSafetySettings, move the SafetyFailMode dropdown onto that page (mirroring it in Sequencer the way auto-park is already mirrored), and when weatherSafetyEnabled flips true with no live source, present a one-time choice rather than a CRITICAL toast.

### Backlash IN ships at 350 steps with method 'Overshoot' - an unmeasured, asymmetric mechanical default applied to every focuser

`default` · settings (imaging / calibration / calibration-library / dark-library / captured-images / image-grading / adaptive-exposure / adaptive-conditions / preflight / autofocus / focus model) · **not yet verified**

**What happens:** A freshly seeded profile has af_backlash_comp_method='Overshoot', af_backlash_in='350', af_backlash_out='0'. Backlash is a per-focuser mechanical property the app cannot know; 350 steps is comparable to the entire default AF sweep (step size 50 x 2 x initial offset 4 = 400 steps) and can exceed the usable travel of small stepper/Crayford focusers, so every AF move is perturbed - or clamped/errored - out of the box. **Expected:** Ship 0/0 (no compensation) and provide a backlash-measurement helper, the way NINA does. A non-zero default silently changes where the focuser lands on the first night on unknown hardware.

**Repro:** Fresh profile (drive_linux.py start --fresh), Skip onboarding, Settings > Equipment > Autofocus. Also sqlite3 .../skotiq.db "select key,value from app_settings where key like 'af_backlash%'".

**Where:** `packages/skotiq_core/lib/src/database/database/default_settings.dart:90-92`

**Proposed fix:** Default af_backlash_in and af_backlash_out to 0, and add a 'Measure backlash' action next to the Backlash compensation dropdown.

### Adaptive Exposure lengthens subs when the sky gets brighter - the opposite of the sub-exposure rule every other tool applies

`ux` · settings (imaging / calibration / calibration-library / dark-library / captured-images / image-grading / adaptive-exposure / adaptive-conditions / preflight / autofocus / focus model) · **not yet verified**

**What happens:** The page states 'Lengthens or shortens each frame automatically when the sky is brighter or darker than your reference, so signal-to-noise stays roughly constant'. The implementation is ratio = 10^((mag_ref - mag_live)/2.5), so a brighter sky (lower mag) multiplies the exposure up. The module's own worked example admits the outcome: 60 s nominal at ref 21.5 becomes 499 s under a Bortle-6 19.2 sky, 'far too long for backyard tracking', and is then clamped. With shipped defaults (ref 21.5, max 600 s) any sky more than ~1 mag brighter than reference pins every sub at the 600 s ceiling all night. SharpCap/NINA/Dr. Glover's rule is the inverse: t proportional to 1/sky-rate, i.e. shorter subs under a brighter sky, to keep the sky from filling the well.

**Expected:** Either invert the scaling to the standard 'swamp read noise / fixed sky ADU' rule, or rename the feature and say plainly that it maximises per-frame SNR at the cost of longer, more loss-prone subs, and default it to a target sky-ADU rather than a magnitude reference.

**Repro:** Settings > Imaging > Adaptive Exposure, read the description and 'How it works' card; compare with the formula at the named line.

**Where:** `native/skotiq_native/sequencer/src/scheduling/adaptive_exposure.rs:332-336 and the module doc at :39-59`

**Proposed fix:** Add a 'target sky background (ADU or e-)' mode that scales t inversely with sky rate, make it the default, and keep the constant-SNR mode as an explicit opt-in.

### Image Grading thresholds cannot be edited until the master toggle is armed, so enabling it arms scale-blind defaults immediately

`ux` · settings (imaging / calibration / calibration-library / dark-library / captured-images / image-grading / adaptive-exposure / adaptive-conditions / preflight / autofocus / focus model) · **not yet verified**

**Proposed fix:** Drop the IgnorePointer, keep only the visual dimming, and switch the absolute HFR gate to arcseconds computed from the optical train.
