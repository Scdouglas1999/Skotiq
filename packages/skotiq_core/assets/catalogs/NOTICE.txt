Nebula light table (nebula_light.csv)

Says which OpenNGC nebulae glow in emission lines and which only reflect
starlight. It is derived from OpenNGC and is shared under the same
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license. It is computed from
these catalogs, retrieved from VizieR (CDS, Strasbourg, France;
doi:10.26093/cds/vizier):

- Sharpless S. 1959, A Catalogue of H II Regions, ApJS 4, 257
  (VizieR VII/20)
- Rodgers A.W., Campbell C.T., Whiteoak J.B. 1960, A catalogue of
  H-alpha emission regions in the southern Milky Way, MNRAS 121, 103
  (VizieR VII/216)
- Anderson L.D. et al. 2014, The WISE catalog of Galactic HII regions,
  ApJS 212, 1 (VizieR J/ApJS/212/1)
- Cederblad S. 1946, Studies of bright diffuse galactic nebulae,
  Medd. Lunds Astron. Obs. Ser. II, 119, 1 (VizieR VII/231)
- Magakian T.Yu. 2003, Merged catalogue of reflection nebulae,
  A&A 399, 141 (VizieR J/A+A/399/141)

This research has made use of the VizieR catalogue access tool, CDS,
Strasbourg, France. The original description of the VizieR service was
published in 2000, A&AS 143, 23.
