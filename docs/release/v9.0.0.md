# Skotiq 9.0.0

Skotiq 9.0 improves the experience of running a rig from the desktop or a linked client. This release brings more consistent equipment and capture information across screens, expands image-format support, improves live stacking and mosaic planning, and corrects location and cloud forecasts.

## Desktop and linked clients

Tonight, Imaging, Analytics and the companion screens follow the host’s run, equipment settings and captured frames more consistently.

Folder selection and file operations use the imaging host’s folders when you are connected remotely. Version-mismatch messages identify which side needs updating, and an older cached dashboard asks for an upgrade or reload.

## Images, live stacking and DepthLock

Camera RAW files can be opened with their sensor metadata and color previews, and XISF support now extends through Science, Darkroom framing and calibration workflows, and Darkroom exports.

Live stacking can start from tonight’s saved frames. Frames from another filter are kept out of the active stack, with an explanation of why they were excluded. Recent Stack & Share results are available on the host and linked clients.

DepthLock now accepts reference frames plate-solved by Skotiq that were previously rejected. References still need to be unprocessed monochrome 16-bit images. Linked photometric calibration uses the image’s complete plate-solve information and the matched stars’ catalog magnitudes.

## Planning and mosaics

Mosaic panel numbers and capture order now agree between Framing, the mosaic wizard, generated sequence targets and saved image headers. Both sequence actions in Framing use the layout you drew. Traversal settings survive saving and reopening a project.

Existing mosaic panel positions and captured-frame counts are preserved when upgrading. Target scheduling and altitude checks also retain learned horizon obstructions alongside your configured altitude limits.

## Sequencing and equipment

Preflight checks ignore disabled steps and groups. A refused meridian flip follows its recovery path without immediately repeating the same refused move. The Flat Wizard counts completed work accurately instead of showing a failed or interrupted attempt as complete.

Manual mount operations check the guider’s state before moving, including paused and lost-lock states. If Skotiq cannot confirm that guiding has stopped when required, it refuses the move.

Camera controls reflect the capabilities of the connected camera, including ISO where supported, and cameras without cooling no longer show a cooling card. A failed capability read preserves the current gain or ISO and offset instead of replacing them with profile defaults. Failed equipment reads remain visibly unavailable rather than looking like valid zero values.

If a libgphoto2 RAW download or decode fails, Skotiq retains the camera original. When the original is still in temporary camera memory, further captures and automatic reconnections are blocked to protect it. Retrieve the original, then explicitly disconnect and reconnect the camera.

## Location and weather

When no observing site is saved, Skotiq obtains an approximate internet-based location at startup. Saved sites and manual edits are preserved. Manual detection now finishes with a result or a useful error instead of waiting indefinitely. Check the detected location and correct it for your observing site.

Cloud forecasts use the National Weather Service’s local forecast grid where covered, with global providers as fallbacks. Weather, planning and linked clients use the same forecast selection and show its source and valid time. Missing or expired data no longer appears as clear skies.

Satellite imagery is labeled separately from the cloud forecast: infrared brightness is not a cloud-cover percentage. Use a local weather-safety sensor for equipment protection.

## Android and responsiveness

Declining notification permission on Android no longer triggers a second prompt during the same startup. A warning explains that alerts cannot wake you while notifications are disabled. Voice-action links open the app correctly, including actions received while it is starting.

Image previews and histogram updates are more responsive during captures and saves. Weather-map tiles appear without the previous fade, reducing delays while navigating the map.

## Updating to 9.0.0

Upgrade the imaging host and all linked clients together. Back up your profile first, and keep separate copies of your image files. To roll back, restore the earlier backup; do not open the upgraded database with an older version.

[Downloads](https://skotiq.com/downloads.html) include Windows x64, Linux x64, and Android APKs for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64. The Android APK can update an existing Skotiq 8.2.1 installation. Keep the complete desktop bundle together when extracting it and install the drivers or vendor libraries required by your equipment. Windows packages are unsigned and may show a SmartScreen warning. macOS and iOS packages are not included.
