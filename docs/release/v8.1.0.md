# Skotiq 8.1.0

Skotiq 8.1.0 brings a complete multi-night Darkroom workflow, per-filter flat capture settings, configurable drift recentering, and substantial reliability and Science improvements developed through repeated testing and real overnight imaging.

## Darkroom: from a night's capture to calibrated masters

- Organize work in **Targets, Calibration, Activity and Edit**, with direct links from session review and notifications.
- Build a target across multiple observing nights in a persistent background job. Review frame footprints, exclusions and the common crop before integration; export a field with uniform coverage.
- Prepare calibration, build targets, deliver results and send a morning message through the night-end workflow. Automation settings remain available in Darkroom.
- Build waiting bias, dark and flat batches from the calibration library. Explicitly selected flat batches wait for their matching calibration; changing approved calibration or the build plan invalidates affected cached integrations.
- Use floating-point calibration and flat normalization, bias-aware dark scaling when applicable, geometric coverage tracking, batch outlier rejection, star-signal weighting, and optional local normalization.
- Choose **1×, 2×, 3× or 4× drizzle**, with configurable drizzle parameters. Higher scales increase processing time and storage; useful resolution gains depend on sampling, seeing and the input offsets.
- Export unclipped linear masters and RGB/LRGB composites, retain build receipts and frame decisions, and register saved masters in the library.
- Improved photometric draft color, gentler automatic noise reduction, channel-aware previews, saved recipe thumbnails, build progress, missing-file explanations, framing review and narrow-window layouts.
- Check working-disk space before integration; reuse registration work and reduce repeated reference-star detection.

## Flat wizard and equipment interface

- Set **gain and offset separately for every filter**, so broadband and narrowband flats can match their lights in one run.
- More direct access to flat capture, a denser and clearer wizard layout, retained saved-frame results and a **Build master flat** action.
- Replace the tall collapsed-rail equipment block with a compact rig-status control that opens a full, readable equipment panel.

## Sequencing, guiding and recovery

- Add a configurable angular-drift trigger, defaulting to **30 arcseconds from the first solved frame after initial centering**. Saved frames are checked for drift, lost stars and obstructions.
- Recover mount tracking-limit stops through the meridian-flip controller, verify tracking and post-flip acquisition, record completed flips promptly, and identify the failing recovery step.
- Recover stalled PHD2 command connections; serialize guider restarts, wait for capture to end, preserve useful guide-star locks through settle timeouts, and keep dither/restart settling distinct.
- Reject frames affected by tracking loss or trails. Frame grading keeps filter and pier-side context, and the UI explains stopped guiding and rejected frames.
- Fix stale stop times and recovery banners, runs that skipped all targets, dawn/obstruction shutdown and calibration ordering, and autofocus baselines after failed sweeps.
- Reduce overnight polling bursts and avoid serial-port probing while a mount is connected. Prevent stale autofocus triggers and improve settle recovery.
- Quality and guiding triggers notify unless the operator configures them to stop the run; dawn behavior follows the sequence's own end-of-night policy.

## Science, First Light and Your Sky

- Move CPU-heavy Science measurements off the UI isolate to keep the interface responsive as frames arrive.
- Use each captured frame's gain-dependent camera response and read noise, grade before publication to First Light/Your Sky, and support reprocessing recorded sessions with ordered outcome history.
- Correct full-WCS projection, parity, reference-pixel handling, catalog overlays, stored rotations and remote propagation; request ASTAP distortion terms and derive field size from the image when needed.
- Improve aperture photometry, comparison-star ensembles, per-filter zero points and transparency, band-specific transformations, and differential AAVSO export.
- Measure real gradients, sky noise, star-shape direction, PSF field structure and optical diagnostics; label sky-to-read-noise measurements accurately.
- First Light uses same-filter, star-flux-scaled references with artifact and subtraction-quality checks, confirms sources across frames, expires stale sightings and explains rejected scans. Chase respects an active sequence's ownership of the mount.
- Link moving-object detections in sky coordinates and offer MPC 80-column or ADES PSV export. These remain candidate measurements for review, not confirmed discoveries.
- Correct duplicate atlas counting, keep filters distinct, queue tile folds, batch index persistence and update remote maps incrementally. Add an atlas enable/disable setting.
- Narration uses settled, completed, usable measurements and the selected photometry target; improve analytics labels and constrained-panel layouts.

## Downloads and upgrade notes

Windows x64, Linux x64 and Android APKs are attached below, with SHA-256 checksums and per-platform update manifests. Each package identifies its source commit. Vendor camera SDKs are not redistributed in the desktop packages; retain/install the drivers required by your equipment.

This is version **8.1.0, build 30**. The release packages are freshly built from the tagged source. The underlying changes have targeted regression tests and real-rig validation; this does not imply validation of every hardware combination. Installing this update does not retroactively repair historical Science records or atlas contributions.
